### Device cautions

ZEISS guide says RNFL/optic-nerve reference comparisons depend on age, with disc size additionally used by some models. Its displayed numeric normal-range table is a specific 69-year-old example, not a universal interval. Reference database is unavailable under age 18 in the cited guide. Do not transplant that example to this catalogue. OCT segmentation errors, imaging quality and model/software changes must be considered. Image-only or qualitative references must not run through a parser that extracts two unrelated numbers as bounds.

## Other entries

### رسم قلب — ECG

Adult references for individual named rows only, not a normal/abnormal classifier for the full tracing:

- **معدل القلب أثناء الراحة — بالغ:** minimum 60, maximum 100, unit bpm. [NHS SVT page](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/supraventricular-tachycardia-svt/) directly states the usual resting range. Athletic physiology, drugs, symptoms, children and pregnancy need clinical context.
- **PR — بالغ:** minimum 120, maximum 200, unit ms. [Original multicentre cohort in BMJ Open](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2021-054238) defines that interval in its ECG measurement methods. Do not apply to children; PR also varies with heart rate.
- **QTc — رجال بالغون:** prefer text `حد الإطالة العملي نحو 450 ms؛ يعتمد على طريقة التصحيح والتقييم السريري` rather than unbounded numeric range. **QTc — سيدات بالغات:** corresponding text near 460 ms. [Original AHA/ACCF/HRS scientific statement, Part IV](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacc.2008.12.014) suggests practical prolonged-QT limits of 460 ms or longer in women and longer than 450 ms in men; short QT is 390 ms or shorter in either sex. Do not label every value below the upper threshold normal. Formula, heart rate, QRS duration, drugs, electrolytes and measured tracing require review. No diagnosis of long-QT syndrome from the reference alone.

The NHS Worcestershire ECG document found during research is **paediatric** and must not be used for adult generic PR/QRS thresholds. No QRS interval was added here without a directly verified adult primary reference.

### Consultation / referral service names

`عـرض باطنـه`, `عـرض قلب`, `عرض اسنان`, `عرض انف و اذن و حنجره` describe requests for specialist consultation, not individual measured analytes. This is an inference from the provided catalogue names; confirm classification in the actual UI. Set **لا ينطبق عليه مدى طبيعي** for these service entries; keep bounds empty. Suggested description: `استشارة تخصصية؛ القرار والنتيجة في تقرير المختص ولا يوجد مدى طبيعي عددي للخدمة.` Do not auto-populate FIT, clearance, normal examination, or clinical outcome. This is classification, not a claim that those specialties have no measurable tests.
