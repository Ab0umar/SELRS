# Attendance UI anti-slop audit 010

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Mode:** AFTER (session override)  
**Scope:** Full attendance module: home/layout/routes, live board, employees/detail, daily operations, leave/holidays, permissions/swaps, reports, shifts, device administration/synchronization, self-service profile, settings, and mappings.  
**Method:** Read-only source audit with 11 independent surface reviews and one deduplicating synthesis pass. No attendance files were edited.

## Summary

The attendance module has a good baseline: concrete routes, responsive card/table variants, shared focus/touch primitives, visible mutation feedback in several areas, report surfaces, and focused live-punch tests. The main risks are **truthful loading/error states**, **keyboard and ARIA semantics**, **mobile target sizing/reflow**, **PDF/export reliability**, and **admin synchronization feedback**.

**27 deduplicated findings:** 14 HIGH, 8 MEDIUM, 5 LOW.

## Findings

### HIGH

#### F-001. Shared DateInput calendar triggers are removed from keyboard order

- **Rules:** R-32
- **Evidence:** `client/src/components/ui/date-input.tsx:L186-L197` sets the calendar Button to `tabIndex={-1}`; the primitive is used throughout attendance date forms and reports.
- **Why:** Keyboard users can reach the text field but not the dedicated picker action.
- **Fix:** Remove the negative tab index, preserve the accessible name and visible focus, and verify focus return after closing.

#### F-002. Shared DateInput calendar triggers are below the touch target

- **Rules:** R-03
- **Evidence:** `date-input.tsx:L186-L195` sets the trigger to `h-9 w-9`, a 36px target.
- **Why:** The shared calendar control is difficult to activate on phones and touch tablets.
- **Fix:** Give the trigger a 44px hit area while retaining a compact visual icon if desired.

#### F-003. Attendance data views conflate loading, failure, and empty results

- **Rules:** R-27
- **Surface:** Home, live board, employees, daily operations, leave, shifts, and admin.
- **Evidence:** `AttendanceHome.tsx:L95-L98,L169-L172` uses unlabeled ellipses and configured-looking shift fallbacks; `LiveBoard.tsx:208-231,353-358,417-422` uses punctuation or non-retry errors. Similar fallback/absent-error branches occur in `DailyView`, `EmployeesList`, `EmployeeDetail`, `LeaveManagement`, `LeaveBalanceReport`, `Holidays`, `ScheduleSwap`, `ShiftAssignments`, `ShiftManagement`, `DeviceSettings`, `AdminDashboard`, `SyncStatus`, and `DeviceConsole`.
- **Why:** Users cannot distinguish pending, successful zero rows, unavailable data, and failure.
- **Fix:** Give every data view explicit announced loading, truthful empty, and actionable error/retry states. Do not coerce unresolved data to zero, false, or empty arrays.

#### F-004. Visible report and admin state controls lack complete tab or pressed semantics

- **Rules:** R-32, C-4
- **Evidence:** `Reports.tsx:L328-L347` visible tabs lack roles, selection, and relationships while correct tabs are hidden at `L373-L398`; `LiveBoard.tsx:330-345` lacks tab semantics and `:236-247` lacks `aria-pressed`; similar gaps exist in ScheduleSwap, ShiftAssignments, MyAttendanceProfile, AdminDashboard, and SettingsHub.
- **Why:** Assistive technology cannot consistently identify selected tabs, modes, weekdays, or toggle state.
- **Fix:** Use real tab/toggle semantics with `aria-selected`/`aria-pressed`, `aria-controls`, stable IDs, roving focus, keyboard handlers, and non-color state cues.

#### F-005. Multiple attendance forms and tables do not reflow at content-fit widths

- **Rules:** R-03, R-35
- **Evidence:** Fixed-width date pairs in `EmployeeDetail.tsx:L464-L475,L714-L738,L812-L847` and `date-input.tsx:L145-L150`; narrow tablet split/table in `LiveBoard.tsx:172-174,326,425-504`; unbreakable fingerprint content in `MonthlyFingerprints.tsx:58-66,208-218,257-315`; fixed/two-state rows in `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx:385,524-684`, `SyncStatus.tsx:115-175`, and `EmpSync.tsx:54-90`.
- **Why:** Phone and tablet widths inherit cramped desktop states, fixed-width children, or horizontal overflow.
- **Fix:** Define intermediate content-driven layout states, stack or wrap controls, allow `min-w-0`, and verify the full width range plus 200% zoom.

#### F-006. Repeated native and icon controls are below the 44px touch target

- **Rules:** R-03
- **Evidence:** Compact controls in `DailyView.tsx` and `ManualPunches.tsx`; smaller overrides in `LeaveManagement.tsx`, `LeaveBalanceReport.tsx`, `ScheduleSwap.tsx`, `ShiftAssignments.tsx`, `UserMappings.tsx`, `Reports.tsx`, `ReportsHub.tsx`, and `AdminDashboard.tsx`.
- **Why:** Many controls are 28px to 40px or have no minimum, making repeated actions hard to hit.
- **Fix:** Use minimum 44px hit areas with spacing and remove smaller local size overrides.

#### F-007. Repeated form controls and icon actions lack accessible names or relationships

- **Rules:** R-32
- **Evidence:** Unnamed icon controls in `EmployeesList.tsx`, `Holidays.tsx`, and `LeaveManagement.tsx`; date labels without `htmlFor`/`id` in `ManualPunches.tsx`; unassociated repeated controls in `ShiftAssignments.tsx` and `UserMappings.tsx`.
- **Why:** Screen readers cannot identify row, day, start, end, employee, save, cancel, and close context.
- **Fix:** Add stable IDs, label/for associations, and contextual Arabic `aria-label`s to repeated and icon-only controls.

#### F-008. Report PDF affordances are print-only or fail silently

- **Rules:** R-26, C-4
- **Evidence:** Print-only popup flows in `Reports.tsx:L74-L105`, `LeaveBalanceReport.tsx:L80-L165`, `PermissionReport.tsx:L86-L135`, and `MonthlyFingerprints.tsx:153-174`; `EmployeeDetail.tsx:L1310-L1493` has no export action.
- **Why:** Users are promised PDF output or print but receive no deterministic download or feedback when popups are blocked; employee detail has no export action.
- **Fix:** Provide a real PDF path or label print accurately, and show actionable pending, success, and failure feedback.

#### F-009. Admin synchronization reports server failure as success or leaves recovery transient

- **Rules:** R-27, R-26, C-4
- **Evidence:** `EmpSync.tsx:12-18,25-33,92-104` ignores `success=false`; `DeviceSettings.tsx:469-493,576-600` lacks reset rejection feedback; other admin failures rely on transient toast/log output.
- **Why:** A server-level failure can be announced as success, while failed operations lose retry context.
- **Fix:** Branch on server success, retain failed operation/input, and expose persistent inline error plus retry.

#### F-010. Profile, employee, and mutation feedback is not announced

- **Rules:** R-27, C-4
- **Evidence:** Mutation results in `EmployeeDetail.tsx:L544-L549,L771-L776,L1047-L1053` and `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx:63-127,466-472,590-596,824-829` lack live/status/alert semantics. `LiveBoard.tsx:361-398,427-460,464-504` has no incoming-punch live region; `DeviceConsole.tsx:386-430` has no `role=log`/`aria-live`.
- **Why:** Assistive technology can miss mutation results, incoming punches, and command output.
- **Fix:** Add concise persistent `role=status`, `role=alert`, or `role=log` announcements without re-announcing full buffers.

#### F-011. Employee identity, profile options, and permission settings can fail open or remain misleading

- **Rules:** R-27, C-4
- **Evidence:** `EmployeeDetail.tsx:L302-L313` leaves unknown IDs in a skeleton; `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx:129-138,695-801` collapses options to empty arrays and `:57-61,398-413` fails open entry permission; `Permissions.tsx:67-69,214-232` shows unresolved settings as checked; `AttendanceHome.tsx:L150-L167` uses configured-looking shift fallbacks.
- **Why:** Unresolved identity/configuration is presented as loading, empty, enabled, or configured data.
- **Fix:** Gate dependent UI on successful identity/configuration queries and expose localized error, empty, and retry states.

#### F-012. Settings, employee, report, and manual-punch tabs have broken keyboard focus relationships

- **Rules:** R-32
- **Evidence:** `SettingsHub.tsx:L47-L85` lacks `onKeyDown`/roving `tabIndex`; `EmployeesHub.tsx:L141-L181` points inactive tabs to unmounted panels; `ReportsHub.tsx:106-134` and `ManualPunches.tsx:165-184` lack consistent roving behavior; `Reports.tsx:L328-L430` exposes hidden rather than visible semantics.
- **Why:** Users receive inconsistent Tab/Arrow behavior and references to panels that do not exist.
- **Fix:** Implement one consistent tab contract with active-only `tabIndex`, Arrow/Home/End movement, and stable panel IDs.

#### F-013. Mobile termination control nests interactive elements

- **Rules:** R-32
- **Evidence:** `EmployeesList.tsx:L779-L793` places a checkbox inside a Button, while the desktop model is separate at `L1187-L1195`.
- **Why:** Nested controls create duplicate focus and activation semantics.
- **Fix:** Use a labeled checkbox outside a button or a single `aria-pressed` button.

#### F-014. Search, pending-request, and shift-management empty states are not truthful

- **Rules:** R-27
- **Evidence:** `EmployeesList.tsx:L633-L637` describes search misses as an empty department; `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx:315-382` hides the pending section when all arrays are empty; `ShiftManagement.tsx:708-709` renders `shifts.map` without loading/empty/error branches.
- **Why:** Successful zero results, filtered no-match, and absent data are silent or described as the wrong condition.
- **Fix:** Use state-specific empty copy and keep a visible empty/status section with a next step.

### MEDIUM

#### F-015. Attendance copy is inconsistent or grammatically unclear

- **Rules:** R-02
- **Evidence:** EmployeesList mixes termination terms; LeaveManagement maps salary and unpaid to one label; Holidays reports edit failures as add failures; ReportsHub/AttendanceLayout narrow report scope; LiveBoard mixes device/channel names. See `EmployeesList.tsx:L633-L637,L488-L502,L777-L793,L867-L874,L1267-L1279`, `LeaveManagement.tsx:L15-L20`, `Holidays.tsx:L41-L49`, `ReportsHub.tsx:L20-L37`, `AttendanceLayout.tsx:L81-L87`, and `LiveBoard.tsx:180-203,267-290`.
- **Why:** Users cannot consistently tell the operation, leave type, report scope, or device identity.
- **Fix:** Use approved domain terms, natural Arabic, truthful operation-specific messages, and canonical device/report vocabulary.

#### F-016. LiveBoard connection and query recovery are not actionable

- **Rules:** R-27, R-26
- **Evidence:** `LiveBoard.tsx:40,66-70,89-110,183-191` shows only connected/disconnected; `:208-231,353-358,417-422` has punctuation/error without local retry; `:511-521` omits `deviceStatus.isError`.
- **Why:** Operators cannot distinguish connecting, reconnecting, rejected, loading, and failed states.
- **Fix:** Expose connection states, named query errors, and relevant retry actions.

#### F-017. Unknown FK direction uses a contradictory exit icon

- **Rules:** R-04, R-31
- **Evidence:** `LiveBoard.tsx:367-373` renders an exit arrow for every non-in value while `:384-390` labels unknown explicitly.
- **Why:** The icon can classify unknown as exit during quick scanning.
- **Fix:** Use a neutral/no directional icon while retaining explicit text.

#### F-018. Duplicate and hidden toolbar/action implementations remain

- **Rules:** R-31
- **Evidence:** `Reports.tsx:L349-L425`, `LeaveBalanceReport.tsx:L152-L198`, `PermissionReport.tsx:151-198`, and `MonthlyFingerprints.tsx:407-443,L485-L503` contain live plus hidden/second identical controls.
- **Why:** Dead or duplicate action markup creates competing maintenance points and scanning noise.
- **Fix:** Keep one shared toolbar/action source of truth.

#### F-019. Report export actions provide no completion or failure feedback

- **Rules:** R-27, R-26
- **Evidence:** `Reports.tsx:52-72` creates CSV without pending/success/failure state; `:74-105` returns for empty rows or blocked popup; `:349-369` has no announcement after activation.
- **Why:** Users cannot confirm export started or learn that the browser prevented it.
- **Fix:** Track and announce outcomes and show actionable failure feedback.

#### F-020. PermissionReport export omits mission counts shown on screen

- **Rules:** R-26, C-4
- **Evidence:** `PermissionReport.tsx:137-146` displays `missionCount`, while CSV `:52-72` and print `:104-118` omit it.
- **Why:** The exported schema is incomplete relative to the reviewed report.
- **Fix:** Align on-screen and exported schemas.

#### F-021. Mappings save feedback precedes confirmed refresh

- **Rules:** R-26
- **Evidence:** `UserMappings.tsx:L28-L35` calls `refetch` without awaiting, clears editing, and shows success; `L47-L55` derives display from `query.data`.
- **Why:** A stale mapping can remain after a success toast or failed refresh.
- **Fix:** Await/invalidate and confirm refresh before announcing success; retain retry state on failure.

#### F-022. Mapping editor loses focus when auto-focused select unmounts

- **Rules:** R-32
- **Evidence:** `UserMappings.tsx:L100-L105` auto-focuses the select; `L139-L145,L271-L277` unmounts it without focus restoration.
- **Why:** Keyboard focus becomes unpredictable after save or cancel.
- **Fix:** Return focus to the row edit trigger after the editor closes.

#### F-023. SyncStatus and EmpSync rows lack narrow-width reflow

- **Rules:** R-03
- **Evidence:** `SyncStatus.tsx:115-175` and `EmpSync.tsx:54-90` keep fields/actions in non-wrapping rows; Button labels are nowrap in `button.tsx:7-8`.
- **Why:** Long labels and fixed port fields crowd phone widths.
- **Fix:** Stack fields and wrap or make actions full-width at the content breakpoint.

#### F-024. AdminDashboard is an unmounted parallel admin surface

- **Rules:** R-24, R-26
- **Evidence:** `AdminDashboard.tsx:8-36` defines a dashboard; `attendance-routes.tsx:13-15` omits it and `:89-117` mounts other admin paths.
- **Why:** Two competing admin compositions can drift while one is effectively dead.
- **Fix:** Choose one canonical protected composition and remove or redirect the other.

### LOW

#### F-025. Decorative, stale, or redundant comments add reading noise

- **Rules:** R-31
- **Evidence:** Stale route count at `attendance-routes.tsx:20-21`; obvious LiveBoard labels at `LiveBoard.tsx:173-175,262,325`; JSX labels throughout EmployeesList, EmployeeDetail, Holidays, ScheduleSwap, ShiftManagement, and admin files.
- **Why:** Comments restate visible structure or preserve stale counts instead of documenting rules or constraints.
- **Fix:** Remove obvious labels and retain only concise intent, API, protocol, performance, or edge-case comments.

#### F-026. Unreachable or generic comments obscure the canonical action path

- **Rules:** R-31
- **Evidence:** `ShiftAssignments.tsx:405-489` is an unreachable duplicate swap branch while `:2065-2228` is the reachable workflow; `AdminDashboard.tsx:41,58,175,266` labels tabs/cards/grids without constraints.
- **Why:** Comments reinforce duplicated structure instead of clarifying the real workflow.
- **Fix:** Remove structural labels and retain only implementation reasons not visible in code.

#### F-027. Shift-change CTA says swap for non-swap request types

- **Rules:** R-15
- **Evidence:** `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx:637-643` offers daily, weekly, monthly, and swap types; `:832-879` always says `إرسال طلب التبديل`.
- **Why:** A daily, weekly, or monthly shift change is not necessarily a swap.
- **Fix:** Make the heading and CTA depend on `requestType`.

## Strengths observed

- AttendanceHome, profile, and several mutation surfaces provide visible retry or pending feedback.
- EmployeesHub and ReportsHub provide real tab semantics and keyboard helpers.
- Shared Button/Input primitives provide focus-visible styling and 44px-class sizing.
- Many list/report surfaces provide responsive cards, contained tables, and text-plus-icon statuses.
- LiveBoard cleans up WebSocket subscriptions and has focused reconnect/deduplication tests.
- Reviewed route declarations are concrete and no duplicate attendance route was found.
- Several report views already include browser print flows and compact card/table renderings.
