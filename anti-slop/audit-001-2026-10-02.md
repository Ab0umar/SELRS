# antislop audit 001 - attendance module UI (2026-10-02)

Mode: AFTER (chosen by the user in this session). Scope: `client/src/features/attendance/*.tsx` and `admin/*.tsx`.
Design read (from `DESIGN.md`): Arabic RTL operational workspace, blue/orange identity, ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.
Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Lock = LOW.
Nothing was checked in a browser; findings come from reading the source. Items marked "verify" need a phone-width or dark-theme look.

Status (2026-10-02): all 15 findings fixed. `tsc` clean, 274 client tests pass. Not yet checked in a browser (phone width and dark theme still need an eyeball).

## HIGH

1. **Invisible icon and arrow on the live-board link** (R-25). `AttendanceHome.tsx:341-349`: the link is `bg-primary text-primary-foreground`, but its icon and arrow use `text-primary`, the same colour as the background.
2. **Zero shown while loading or on error** (R-27, R-17). `AttendanceHome.tsx:109` declares `isLoading` and never uses it, so late/yesterday/inside render `0` until data arrives or if the query fails. `LiveBoard.tsx` has no loading state at all; `Reports.tsx` and `PermissionReport.tsx` have no error handling. The summary also returns `presentToday` and `absentToday`, which the home page never shows.
3. **Light-only colours, no dark variants** (R-34). Fixed palette classes with zero `dark:` rules: `admin/DeviceConsole.tsx` (61), `LiveBoard.tsx` (29), `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx` (19), `admin/DeviceSettings.tsx` (17), `MonthlyFingerprints.tsx` (12), `admin/SyncStatus.tsx` (8), `AttendanceHome.tsx` (8), e.g. `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700` at `AttendanceHome.tsx:186`. Verify contrast in the dark theme.
4. **Classes that do not exist** (R-35). Tailwind has no such shades, so the border or text silently disappears: `border-rose-350` x3, `border-sky-350` x2, `border-emerald-350` (`admin/DeviceSettings.tsx`), `border-rose-250` x2 and `text-sky-850` (`LiveBoard.tsx`).
5. **Text at 9-10px** (R-03). About 130 uses of `text-[8px]`/`text-[9px]`/`text-[10px]`: `admin/DeviceSettings.tsx` 36, `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx` 28, `LiveBoard.tsx` 23, `ShiftManagement.tsx` 13, `AttendanceHome.tsx` 12. Arabic at that size is hard to read on a phone.
6. **Grids that keep 3-4 columns on a phone** (R-03, verify). `grid-cols-3`/`grid-cols-4` with no breakpoint: `AttendanceHome.tsx:272`, `EmployeeDetail.tsx:347`, `EmployeesList.tsx:692`, `LeaveBalanceReport.tsx:430` (4 columns), `LeaveManagement.tsx:417`, `Permissions.tsx:455`, `ShiftAssignments.tsx:984` and `:1821`, `admin/DeviceSettings.tsx:420` and `:524`.
7. **Small tap targets** (R-03). Buttons at `h-6`/`h-7`/`h-8` or `size="sm"`: `ShiftAssignments.tsx` 24, `EmployeesList.tsx` 24, `LeaveBalanceReport.tsx` 11, `EmployeeDetail.tsx` 11, `ScheduleSwap.tsx` 8, `Holidays.tsx` 8. Below the 44px touch minimum.

## MEDIUM

8. **Glow and endless pulses on status dots** (R-13, R-19, R-31). `AttendanceHome.tsx:378-381` (ping ring plus `shadow-[0_0_8px…]`), `LiveBoard.tsx:198` and `:284`, `admin/DeviceSettings.tsx:176-284` (glowing pills with `animate-ping`), `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx:321`, `:337`, `:362` (pulsing dots that mark no state). The MOTION dial is 1.
9. **Emoji in UI text** (R-04). `AttendanceHome.tsx:373` "📡 حالة الأجهزة (Live Radar)" (also mixes an English label into Arabic), `EmployeesList.tsx:359` and `ScheduleSwap.tsx:903` use "⚠️".
10. **Heavy shadows** (R-12). `admin/SyncStatus.tsx` and `admin/EmpSync.tsx`: `shadow-lg shadow-slate-100/80 … hover:shadow-xl` on cards and `shadow-md hover:shadow-lg` on buttons; `AttendanceHome.tsx:371` device card `shadow-xl`.
11. **Uppercase and letter-spacing on Arabic headings** (R-06). About 16 headings use `uppercase tracking-wider/widest` (`AttendanceHome.tsx:271`, `:308`, `LiveBoard.tsx:327`, `MyAttendanceProfile.tsx:378`, `:485`, `:603`, `admin/DeviceConsole.tsx:183`, `:226`). Uppercase does nothing in Arabic and letter-spacing breaks the joined letters.
12. **Decorative gauges and a misleading bar** (R-31, R-17). `AttendanceHome.tsx:276-292`: the three rings (`border-4 border-t-amber-500` and similar) encode nothing and look like loading spinners. The two "timeline" bars at `:230` and `:254` show how much of the shift window has elapsed, not attendance, but sit next to "N موظف حاضر حالياً".

## LOW

14. **Native `confirm()` for deletions** (R-20). 19 uses (`Permissions.tsx`, `LeaveManagement.tsx`, `EmployeesList.tsx` 4 each, plus `ManualPunches.tsx`, `Holidays.tsx`, `admin/DeviceSettings.tsx`, `ShiftManagement.tsx`). Unstyled, not part of the design system, and not RTL-aware.
15. **Entrance animation on every tab switch** (R-19). `animate-in fade-in slide-in-from-bottom-2 duration-300` on the three hub pages (`EmployeesHub.tsx:163`, `ReportsHub.tsx:180`, `SettingsHub.tsx:79`) while MOTION is 1.
