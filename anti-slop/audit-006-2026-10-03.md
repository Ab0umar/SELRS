## HIGH

1. **Em dashes in UI text** (R-02, forbidden in any text). 90 lines use `—` as a connector, in four shapes:
   - Range labels: `${from} — ${to}` in `PermissionReport.tsx:89,102,245`, `AbsentReport.tsx:79`, `PayrollReport.tsx:115`, `SalaryPenalties.tsx:62`, `ShiftPayroll.tsx:198`. Fix: "من X إلى Y".
   - Title and subtitle joins: "الرواتب الأساسية — {periodLabel}" (`PayrollReport.tsx:2383,2813,3528`), "التأخيرات — {periodLabel}" and the other penalty tabs (`SalaryPenalties.tsx`, 12), "أيام الغياب — …", "قناة البث المباشر — FK" (`LiveBoard.tsx:180,267`), "توزيع الوردية — {empName}" (`EmployeesList.tsx:156,345`). Fix: a colon ("التأخيرات: …") or a comma.
   - Print and document titles built from three parts ("كشف الرواتب — {section} — {period}", `PayrollReport.tsx` 20 lines, `SalaryPenalties.tsx` 12 lines, `ShiftPayroll.tsx:258`). Fix: colon or " | " between parts.
   - Mid-sentence asides: `ShiftAssignments.tsx:1242-1248` ("تتابع متكرر — يبدأ من تاريخ البداية"), `EmployeesList.tsx:359`, `ManualPunches.tsx:130`, `SalaryPenalties.tsx:1249,1327`, `ShiftStaff.tsx:221`, `PayrollReport.tsx:4434`, `UserMappings.tsx:119,219`. Fix: comma, colon or a new sentence.
   - Select placeholders wrapped in dashes: "— اختر الوردية —" (16 options in `EmployeeDetail`, `MyAttendanceProfile`, `ScheduleSwap`, `ShiftAssignments`, `UserMappings`, `EmployeesList`). Fix: "اختر الوردية".
2. **The empty-value glyph `—`** (R-02). 86 uses of a lone `—` for "no value" in table cells and cards (`"—"`, `|| "—"`), including the new dashboard figures when a query fails. It is the same character the rule bans. Recommended fix: a plain hyphen `-`, or "غير محدد" where the cell needs to say why it is empty. This is a decision (see the end).

## MEDIUM

3. **"تم إضافة" is the wrong form** (grammar). 10 uses: `EmployeesList.tsx:105`, `Holidays.tsx:47,65`, `LeaveManagement.tsx:91`, `Permissions.tsx:93`, `SalaryBasics.tsx:729`, `SalaryPenalties.tsx:115,137`, and others. The standard form is "تمت إضافة". Related forms in the same file set ("تم إنشاء", "تم حذف", "تم تعديل", "تم رفض") are correct.
4. **Padding "بنجاح" on 9 of about 100 success toasts.** "تم الاعتماد بنجاح", "تم تعيين الجدول الأسبوعي بنجاح", "تم تبديل الورديات بنجاح", "تم تطبيق التغيير اليومي للوردية بنجاح" sit next to "تم الاعتماد" and "تم الحفظ", which say the same without the word. A success toast already means success. Fix: drop "بنجاح" (R-36, filler).
5. **Two voices for the same prompt.** Validation toasts use both "الرجاء اختيار X" (8) and "اختر X" (8 or more): "الرجاء اختيار الموظف" vs "اختر موظفاً" for the same missing field. Pick one (the short imperative matches the rest of the UI) and apply it to all.
6. **English left in Arabic screens** (project language rule). `ShiftStaff.tsx`: "Doctor", "Technician", "Active", "Inactive", "Manual" (`:442,443,517,518,568,720`), select options "— None —" and "— No account —" (`:482,501`), toast "Fill all fields" (2 uses), and a raw `endTime` label (`:115`). The print header "SELRS Medical Center" in `PayrollReport.tsx:897` and `ShiftSchedule.tsx:765` is the brand name and stays.
7. **Generic error toasts** (R-27 spirit). 68 toasts read "خطأ: " followed by the raw server message, so they never say what failed ("خطأ: " after a delete or a save is the same text). Fix per action: "تعذر حفظ الراتب: …", "تعذر حذف الجزاء: …". This is 68 small edits and needs the action name at each site.

## LOW

8. **Ellipsis style.** 55 loading texts use the single character `…`; 3 still use three dots (`...`). Unify on `…`.
9. **Two empty-state phrasings** for "nothing found": "لا توجد X في هذه الفترة", "لا توجد X تطابق البحث", "لا يوجد X". These are different cases (no data vs no match) and read fine; listed only to record that they were checked.
