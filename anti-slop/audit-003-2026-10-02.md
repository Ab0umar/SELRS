## HIGH

1. **Payroll report shows an empty report while loading or on failure** (R-17, R-27). `PayrollReport.tsx:121-128,190`: `getPayroll` is queried for المركز and العيادة, and the rows are `query.data ?? []`. There is no `isLoading` or `isError` branch for these two queries (the other 6 queries have none either), so a slow or failed request looks like "no payroll", which is the worst thing to show on a money screen.
2. **Invented status on the dashboard** (R-17, R-18, R-31). `SalaryDashboard.redesigned.tsx:142-158`: a pulsing green dot (`animate-ping` plus glow shadow) next to "قواعد التدقيق التلقائي: جاهز ومطابق". Nothing checks any audit rule; the text is hard-coded, so it claims a verification that does not exist.
3. **No error state on three data pages** (R-27). `SalaryDashboard.redesigned.tsx:23` (`monthSummary`), `AbsentReport.tsx:62` (`getAbsentDays`) and the PayrollReport queries have no error branch. The dashboard prints `0.00 ج.م` for the totals if the query fails.
4. **Invisible or low-contrast text on the dashboard** (R-25). `SalaryDashboard.redesigned.tsx:98-122`: `text-amber-400` on `bg-amber-500/10`, and `text-rose-400` on `bg-rose-500/10`, both on a light card. Contrast is well under 4.5:1 in the light theme (the `-300` badges are worse). The big commissions figure at `:57` is `text-amber-500` on white.
5. **Tables that clip on a phone** (R-03). `SalaryPenalties.tsx:1124,1208,1290`: three expanded-row tables (`w-full ... overflow-hidden`) with no scroll wrapper, so columns are cut off. `CommissionPools.tsx:133,623` use `min-w-[620px]`/`min-w-[500px]` (check that a scroll wrapper exists). `ShiftPayroll.tsx:385` is `hidden lg:block` with a card list below, which is fine.
6. **Fixed 3-4 column grids on a phone** (R-03). `SalaryDashboard.redesigned.tsx:73` (`grid-cols-3 gap-6` with 56px rings), `SalarySettings.tsx:1070` (a 3-column `TabsList` whose labels are "نسب الحضور العامة (المركز)" / "شرائح خصم التأخير (لكل يوم)" / "إعدادات الموظفين الخاصة", so each wraps into 3 lines at 375px), `CommissionPools.tsx:962`, `PayrollReport.tsx:3004,3031`, `ShiftStaff.tsx:144` (`grid-cols-7`).
7. **Text below 12px** (R-03). About 110 uses of `text-[8px]`...`text-[11px]`: `SalarySettings.tsx` 22, `CommissionPools.tsx` 16, `ShiftSchedule.tsx` 15, `ShiftStaff.tsx` 14, `SalaryPenalties.tsx` 10, `SalaryBasics.tsx` 9, `PayrollReport.tsx` 7, `SalaryDashboard.redesigned.tsx` 7 (the gauge captions at 10px and the badges at 9px).
8. **Icon buttons with no accessible name** (R-33). `ShiftSchedule.tsx:863,877,895`: print, previous month and next month use `title` only. Prefer `aria-label` (screen readers announce `title` inconsistently, and touch users never see it). The `EmployeeFunds` icon buttons already have `aria-label`.
