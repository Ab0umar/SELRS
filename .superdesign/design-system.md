# عيون الشروق medical reports design system

عيون الشروق is an Arabic RTL staff app for ophthalmology workflows. The medical reports hub at `/reports` uses a navy brand (`#003D82`, with `#00355f` / `#003d9b` accents), Cairo typography, and shadcn-style Tailwind primitives. Reports are operational documents: prioritize fast patient selection, clear six-tab navigation, compact OD/OS clinical grids, strong borders and labels, and predictable A4 portrait printing.

The redesign should preserve authenticated routing, patient-id URL synchronization, and print-only document chrome while making the report hub easy for staff to scan and reuse.
